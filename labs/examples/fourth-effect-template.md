# Fourth Effect Template

You've successfully hacked your own Moodle Lab. Now it's time to get creative!

Design a fun effect using the same PoC (it can be harmful or just a prank). Surprise us with something different from the three in-class examples!

Some ideas:
- Give a fictional course a ridiculous name or description.
- Replace a test announcement with a funny message.
- Phishing a test account you create in your own lab

## What to submit

Show us:

1. **Before** — What did Moodle look like originally?
2. **After** — What happened after your prank?
3. **Reset** — Show that you can restore everything.
4. **How** — Include your scripts or commands and briefly explain what you changed.

This challenge is worth **10% of your base grade**. If you don't complete it, your base grade is capped at 90; bonus points can still bring your final score up to 100.
